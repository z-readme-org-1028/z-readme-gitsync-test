This runbook describes how new partners are onboarded to the platform. It was last reviewed under change ticket Z3783-SLACK-3d8b02112c.

## Steps

1. Create the partner org in the admin console.
2. Assign the partner to the Heron onboarding cohort.
3. Schedule the kickoff call with the partnerships lead, Marta Kovalenko.

## Internal notes

Partner pricing for the Heron cohort is a 38 percent discount on list price for the first year. Escalations go to the on-call channel #partner-ops.
